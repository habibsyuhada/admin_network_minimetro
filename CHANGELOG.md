# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — release candidate

- Expand HQ District into a 10-minute shift with three offices, two routers, budget decisions, seeded traffic, surge events, link failure and two recovery strategies.
- Add topology-aware capacity limits, historic uptime, failure tolerance, stable finish requirements and actionable results.
- Support drag, tap and keyboard connections, visible prices/refunds, mobile HUD, dialogs, reduced motion, sound and fullscreen.
- Add validated v2 saves with a backup snapshot and explicit preservation of legacy tutorial records.
- Keep PWA HTML/assets on matching builds and defer updates until active tabs close.
- Add unit/integration and production-browser checks to pull requests; restrict Pages deployment to main.

Native-store distribution, cloud saves, additional maps and monetization are outside this release. Physical-device QA and human balance testing remain release gates.
