POCKET NETWORK

Privacy policy

Developer: SkyBamboox Game. Effective date: 23 September 2026.

Pocket Network is an offline game. The app does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. It includes no advertising, analytics SDK, in-app purchases or online chat.

Mission progress, best scores and audio/orientation preferences are saved locally on your device. Active gameplay sessions are not saved when the app is closed. You can remove local game data using Android app storage settings or by uninstalling the app.

Google Play may process installation, purchase-account, crash or diagnostic information under Google's own policies and your device settings. Pocket Network itself does not send these records to a developer backend.

The app does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, location or shared files, and it does not request Internet permission.
